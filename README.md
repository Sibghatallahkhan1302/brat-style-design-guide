# Brat Style Design Guide

The brat look is a bold, minimal design trend built on flat colour and plain text. If you want to try it without opening a design app, a [brat generator](https://bratgeneratortool.com/) lets you type your text and get a styled image in seconds. This guide covers the basics.

## The core look

Most brat-style designs use a flat lime green background with plain, lowercase text in a simple sans-serif font. The text is often slightly blurred or squeezed to give it a raw, low-fi feel.

## Colour tips

Keep the palette tiny. One strong background colour and one text colour are enough. High contrast makes the words readable on small screens.

## Text and layout tips

Use short phrases, keep the text centred or left-aligned, and leave generous empty space around it. Avoid decorative fonts, because the charm of the style is in its simplicity.

## Where to use it

Profile pictures, story posts, playlist covers, memes and party invitations all suit this style.
